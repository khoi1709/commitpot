# Consent-based usability exercise — proposal, not recruitment

**Status:** proposed protocol only. No participants were recruited, no exercise was run, and no results exist. Writing this protocol does not authorize an invitation, event, public release, transaction, or data collection.

## Scope and safeguards

The exercise asks whether a person can understand and complete the local demonstration flow. It cannot establish reduced no-shows, financial benefit, security, or public-network readiness. Use only the clearly labelled Local demonstration with synthetic wallets/receipts, or a separately authorized disposable local Anvil environment. Never use Fuji, real money, a real wallet, a real key, or a participant's own tokens. Participation is optional; declining or stopping has no penalty. Explain that deposits in any separately approved test-token flow can be lost under its fixed rules before asking for consent.

A future organizer must obtain separate permission before inviting anyone and must confirm the environment, script, observers, accessibility accommodations, retention period, and deletion process first. This document itself is not an invitation or consent form. Do not recruit minors or collect sensitive information.

## Proposed session

1. Explain the prototype, test-token-only scope, verifier role, limitations, what will be observed, and the right to stop. Ask for informed agreement before beginning.
2. Ask the participant to describe the deposit, attendance, no-show and refund rules in their own words. Correct misunderstandings before any wallet-like action; do not treat quiz accuracy as product validation.
3. Offer a prepared local scenario: inspect a pot, join using the clearly synthetic demo account, read a personal QR example, complete an explicitly human-attested check-in, and inspect the final claim/refund state. Do not imply a QR proves presence or ownership.
4. Allow ordinary hesitation and questions. Do not coach through an error unless the participant asks; record that help was given. Stop immediately on request or if the participant believes real funds or a real wallet are involved.
5. At the end, explain that the exercise is not a live deployment and ask whether notes may be retained under the pre-disclosed retention policy. Delete declined or withdrawn session notes.

## Minimal observation record

Record only a random session label, task outcome, whether assistance was requested, broad failure category, and participant-reported clarity. Do not record names, contact details, wallet addresses, QR contents, screen recordings, biometrics, demographic profiles, private keys, or seed phrases. Do not attach transaction hashes or personal identities. If an unexpected identifier appears in a capture, stop and remove it before retaining any artifact.

For each task, define start and end consistently before the first session. Report counts as **completed / started** and **claims / eligible fixture wallets** where applicable. Include stopped, failed, pending, and abandoned attempts in the denominator notes. If timing is approved, report sample size and median plus minimum–maximum; distinguish participant task time from wallet/RPC waiting. Do not aggregate unlike mock, local-chain, and public-network evidence. With no observations, publish no completion rate, timing, usability, attendance, or impact result.

## Reporting and limits

A future summary should state the environment, number of consenting sessions, tasks attempted, help given, failures and withdrawals, missing data, retention/deletion date, and the exact scope of any timing measure. Small convenience samples are descriptive only. Test tokens have no real-world stake, so successful completion cannot demonstrate a real attendance effect. A causal attendance claim would require a separately designed, authorized comparative study and is outside this protocol.

**Tiếng Việt — tóm tắt:** Đây chỉ là đề xuất thử khả năng sử dụng; chưa tuyển người, chưa thực hiện và chưa có kết quả. Không coi tài liệu này là lời mời tham gia hay biểu mẫu đồng thuận. Cần xin phép riêng trước khi mời người tham gia. Chỉ dùng token và ví tổng hợp; không dùng tiền thật, ví/khóa của người dùng hoặc Fuji. Giải thích rõ quy tắc và quyền dừng trước khi bắt đầu. Chỉ ghi kết quả tác vụ tối thiểu; không lưu tên, địa chỉ ví, nội dung QR, hình ảnh, khóa riêng hay cụm từ khôi phục. Không có dữ liệu thì không đưa ra tỷ lệ hoàn thành, số liệu thời gian, tác động hay kết luận giảm vắng mặt.
