# CommitPot — eight-slide pitch outline (English)

These short text slides mirror the eight panels in the [offline HTML deck](<../../output/pitch/commitpot-en.html>). They are speaker prompts, not a claim of a recorded pitch, accessible published slide file, or live deployment. Use evidence and limitations from the [evidence ledger](<README.md>).

1. **CommitPot.** Fixed deposits, accountable human check-in, inspectable payouts. An opt-in workshop agreement; no claim of better attendance. Test tokens only.
2. **A small workshop, a clear bargain.** A fictional club has limited seats and materials. Compare organizer-held transfers with contract-fixed rules. Trade-offs: wallet prompts, gas and a trusted verifier. No recruited event or study.
3. **Agree before depositing.** Set token, stake, verifier and deadlines. Approve and deposit before registration closes. A named human checks presence. After close, anyone can finalize; eligible beneficiaries claim. No attendance means joined wallets reclaim stake.
4. **One historical local outcome.** Saved Anvil 31337, pot 3: five synthetic wallets deposited 10 each; four checked in and claimed 12.5 each; total obligation and isolated balance reconciled to zero. Not five people, Fuji, or current-candidate acceptance; complete receipt-hash ledger is absent.
5. **No admin withdrawal is not no trust.** Rules fix eligibility and payment destination. The verifier, wallet, RPC, token, and source/deployment match remain trusted. QR is not proof of presence or ownership. No cancellation, verifier replacement, disputes, expiry, or sweep.
6. **Evidence has tiers.** Mock demonstrates interface only. Historical Anvil demonstrates a local transaction flow with synthetic wallets. Fuji release remains pending. A denial assertion is not a mined revert; label read-only simulation honestly.
7. **Measure usability, not invented impact.** A consent-based test-token exercise is only proposed. Keep people, fixture wallets, tasks and attempts distinct; retain failures and denominators. Test tokens cannot prove fewer real-world no-shows.
8. **Narrow product, explicit next gate.** Inspect the current candidate and fresh local evidence. Separately authorize any Fuji release, hosting, recording, accessible submission slides, or consented exercise. No predicted judging score or external outcome is claimed.
