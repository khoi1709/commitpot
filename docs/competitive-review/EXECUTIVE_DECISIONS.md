# Executive recommendation — what to improve, not what to imitate

## Recommendation

Keep CommitPot a **small, inspectable offline-attendance agreement**. The strongest competitive upgrade is a source-bound public Avalanche proof and an understandable real event, supported by reliable recovery—not AI, yield, NFTs or more chains.

Six exact GPT-6 Astra Max specialists were used: closest social-finance comparison, judge evidence, onboarding/demo, local technical gaps, product strategy, and fresh independent priority adjudication. Their reports distinguish public self-identification from private actual submissions, README claims from observed pages, and source inspection from execution.

## Priority decisions

1. **Correct the active decision path.** New camera input must clear any earlier participant and presence attestation; delayed lookup cannot restore it. Signing/preflight failures are not broadcasts. Corrupt pending records and failed storage cleanup must not erase uncertain evidence or encourage a second payment. Successful reconciliation must record the same real confirmation as the ordinary receipt path.
2. **Make proof public and exact.** After separately approved Fuji/hosting, a wallet-free judge entry shows one event, exact release/deployment identity, five10-token deposits, four12.5 payouts, no-show rejection and all-no-show refund evidence. Historical receipts, local simulations and current on-chain reads have different labels. Never equate our source HEAD with deployed code without verifying the match.
3. **Make the event recognizable.** Use an approved public demonstration title/purpose/broad venue/timezone/verifier label, clearly non-authoritative metadata bound to chain/core/pot. Rename the global “Your events” feed honestly. Do not expose private meeting or participant details.
4. **Make the verifier desk work at the door.** Keep EN/VI and the existing identity; prioritize scanner/manual entry, exact participant, attestation and confirmed status above secondary chronology. Explain AVAX gas separately from token deposit. Preserve read-only access without an extension and during unrelated list/wallet failure.
5. **Prove assurance and usefulness.** Add targeted stateful accounting/token-failure/recovery assertions and reproducible CI, not badge inflation. Show full receipt references/explorer links and exact test/release identity. Complete a consenting test-token user exercise, accessible slides and a2–3-minute evidence-first video; report actual sample/limits, not fabricated no-show improvement.

## Competitive pattern summary

- **StudyStake:** strongest thematic overlap, but its course/yield/principal-return subject to vault loss is a different bargain. Borrow understandable scenario and proof navigation, not vault/oracle/guardian mechanisms.
- **MissionPay:** strongest observed judge-evidence pattern. Public page pairs historical positive/negative controls and discloses deployed/local version differences. Borrow that honesty and verifiability, not its AI/passkey/ICM architecture.
- **Pact:** clear mobile QR/payment entry and test-token boundary; claimed gasless/latency not independently exercised. Improve wallet/gas guidance before introducing account abstraction.
- **AvaHui:** concrete Vietnamese use-case story. Do not adopt unverified zero-risk, yield or timing claims.
- **AgentLeash:** guest inspection before privileged operations. The observed page still had loading data; it does not certify successful settlement.

## Decisions not silently made by this research

Cancellation, verifier replacement/failure remedies, disputes, no-loss/sponsored rewards, shared metadata authentication/privacy, wallet custody/paymaster/relayer services and actual Fuji/hosting authority require explicit decisions. Zero-attendance refund does not resolve unfair partial attendance after verifier loss.

## Evidence limitations

Actual organizer submissions are private. Public repos are self-identified or candidates, not confirmed finalists or ranks. GitHub rate limiting prevented SHA-pinned opponent contract/tests/CI inspection; no alternate backend/account/IP workaround used. This is a useful qualified product/evidence comparison plus source-grounded review of our own code—not a complete competitor security audit. No new builds, tests, deployments, app changes or commits occurred in this research. The only working-tree additions are this research folder.
